Who I Am

Educator.Designer. Problem-Solver.

I'm a learning experience designer and educator who believes that well-structured knowledge changes lives. With a background spanning classroom instruction, virtual assistance, and digital tool integration, I've spent years figuring out how to make learning actually work — not just look good on a slide deck.

My approach sits at the intersection of instructional design, learner psychology, and practical technology. I don't just create content — I engineer pathways. Whether I'm building a curriculum from scratch, redesigning a broken onboarding flow, or translating dense technical material into digestible modules, my goal is the same: clarity that drives change.

I'm currently targeting roles in Instructional Design, Learning Experience Design, and EdTech where I can bring both pedagogical rigour and creative problem-solving to teams building the future of learning.

Project 01 · Data Analysis & Learning Analytics

Learner Performance Analysis & Intervention Dashboard

Problem
Raw assessment scores were stored across three separate term sheets with no way to identify recurring learning gaps, compare cohort trends, or flag learners needing targeted support.
Solution
Built a multi-term Excel dashboard that transforms 1,150 subject-score records across 46 learners and 17 subjects into clear performance insights, support tiers, and actionable intervention recommendations.
Outcome
↑ Cohort avg 87.3% · 9 priority learner-terms flagged · IGBO identified as recurring class-wide gap · 4-tier intervention plan produced

Virtual Classroom Experience Design for Online Tutoring

How I Work

A Structured Design Process

Every learning experience I build follows a deliberate workflow — grounded in learner needs, not assumptions.

01
Understand the Learner
Needs analysis, learner profiling, and context mapping before a single slide is built.
02
Define Learning Goals
Clear, measurable objectives aligned to business or learner outcomes — no vague aims.
03
Design the Structure
Logical content architecture, sequencing, pacing, and pathway planning.
04
Develop Content
Writing, scripting, and building materials across formats — text, visual, audio, interactive.
05
Test & Improve
Pilot testing, feedback collection, data review, and iterative refinement until it works.
